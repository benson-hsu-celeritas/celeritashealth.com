## Worth doing
A body of work whose thesis is "do not trust unverified claims" cannot carry unverified claims of its own.

## Build or buy
Built a claims-to-artifacts manifest: every number in every manuscript mapped to the file that evidences it. Anything unmapped is "from the summary", not "verified".

## Rules
Only numbers that regenerate from a deposit go public. Retired figures never reappear, in any form.

## Is it working
Yes. The papers that remain rest on logged facts and public deposits. The cost was two manuscripts and a month.
