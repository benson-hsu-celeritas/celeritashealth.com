## Worth doing
Most acutely ill children are seen in departments the ideal step was not written for.

## Build or buy
This one is a proposal. It describes the record a system should keep: what it recommended, what the site could do, and what was substituted or dropped.

## Rules
A safe substitution and an unsafe deletion look identical on the screen without a record.

## Is it working
No one has measured it yet. I am asking the readiness bodies and the buyers to start.
