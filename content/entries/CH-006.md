## Worth doing
Most acutely ill children are seen in departments the ideal step was not written for.

## Build or buy
Proposes the record and scores any system could be held to: what was recommended, what the site could do, what was substituted or dropped.

## Rules
A safe substitution and an unsafe deletion look identical on the screen without a record.

## Is it working
Not yet measured by anyone. This card asks readiness bodies and buyers to score it.
