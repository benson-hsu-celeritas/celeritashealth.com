## Worth doing
Community and rural hospitals lack current pediatric pathways. A model can draft one in minutes. The question was whether a library of them could be made safe enough to hand to a physician for adjudication.

## Build or buy
Built. Claude Sonnet 4.5 generated, with citations omitted by instruction. Each pathway then passed deterministic PubMed citation verification, review by a different model (Claude Haiku 4.5), a deterministic dosing range-check, and comparison against peer practice. A disposition engine assigned approved, review or hold.

## Rules
Each pathway opens with a header: when it was generated, when it was last verified, when it is due for review, what the gates found, what was patched, and where it conflicts with another pathway. Anything that cannot be verified is held for review; it is not approved. Every pathway remains a candidate for physician adjudication; none is clinical.

## Is it working
The deterministic layers held. The citations did not (CH-002). The second model missed an omission that the external comparison caught (CH-003). Seven of the numbers I first reported did not survive reconciliation (CH-004).
