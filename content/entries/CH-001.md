## Worth doing
Community and rural hospitals lack current pediatric pathways. A model can draft one in minutes. The question was whether a library of them could be made safe enough to hand to a physician for adjudication.

## Build or buy
Built. Claude Sonnet 4.5 generated, with citations omitted by instruction. Each pathway then passed deterministic PubMed citation verification, review by a different model (Claude Haiku 4.5), a deterministic dosing range-check, and comparison against peer practice. A disposition engine assigned approved, review or hold.

## Rules
Every pathway carries a provenance header: generated, last verified, review-by date, phase results, patches applied, cross-pathway conflicts. Fail-closed: anything unverifiable escalates rather than approves. All pathways remain candidates for physician adjudication; none are clinical.

## Is it working
What held: the deterministic layers. What broke: see CH-002 (citations) and CH-003 (the omission the model reviewer missed). What did not survive reconciliation: see CH-004.
