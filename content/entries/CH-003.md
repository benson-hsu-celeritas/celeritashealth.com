## Worth doing
The errors that matter in clinical content are omissions, not fabrications. Whether model-based review detects them is an open question.

## Build or buy
The pipeline already had both layers. This card reports what each caught in one bounded case with model-independent ground truth.

## Rules
Validation of generative clinical content should be anchored to external standards. A reviewing model from the same family as the generator shares its blind spots.

## Is it working
Hypothesis-generating. The next step named in the paper: score every validation layer against a labeled set of safety facts across all pathways.
