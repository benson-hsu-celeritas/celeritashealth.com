## Worth doing
In a pathway the dangerous error is the line that is missing. I did not know whether a reviewing model would notice one.

## Build or buy
Nothing new was built. The pipeline already had a model reviewer and an external comparison; this is what each caught on one case where the right answer is known without any model.

## Rules
Check generated clinical content against something outside the model. Here the reviewer and the generator came from the same model family. One case cannot say whether that is why.

## Is it working
This is one case, not a rate. The next step is to score each layer against a labeled list of safety facts across all the pathways.
