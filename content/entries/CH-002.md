## Worth doing
Fabricated citations are the failure that ends a clinical AI pilot. Measuring the rate in a deployed library, with a reproducible method, is the evidence a buyer can check.

## Build or buy
Built: omit-then-verify. Instruct the model to omit identifiers; retrieve them deterministically from NCBI E-utilities; accept a match only on title-token overlap; otherwise mark unverified. Code and the verification script are in the open deposit.

## Rules
Unverifiable is not fabricated. Operators need the taxonomy: verified, topic mismatch, not found. The not-found tail is mostly legitimate sources outside PubMed.

## Is it working
Yes, by a controlled measurement with a confidence interval, reproducible from public bibliographic data alone. Journal status appears only in the status line; numbers may change after review.
